Planning a trip to Canada? Whether you're coming to see the breathtaking Rockies, explore vibrant cities, or visit family and friends, you may need a Temporary Resident Visa (TRV), commonly known as a tourist or visitor visa. This official document allows you to enter and stay in Canada for a temporary period, typically up to six months.

Key Requirements for a Canadian Tourist Visa:

  • A Valid Passport: Your passport must be valid for the duration of your stay in Canada.
  • Proof of Funds: You must demonstrate that you have enough money to support yourself and any accompanying family members during your visit.
  • Strong Ties to Your Home Country: You need to show that you have strong reasons to return home. This can include a job, family members, financial assets, or property.
  • No Criminal or Immigration Convictions: You must not have a criminal record or any history of immigration-related violations.
  • Good Health: You must be in good health. A medical examination may be required.
  • Purpose of Visit: You need to have a clear reason for your visit, such as tourism, visiting family, or attending a conference. This is often supported by a detailed travel itinerary or a Letter of Invitation.
